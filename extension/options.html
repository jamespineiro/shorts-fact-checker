🔎 Shorts Fact Checker

When you watch a YouTube Short, this checks what it claims and shows a clear ✅ / ⚠️ / ❌ label in the top-left corner.

Get one at console.anthropic.com. It is stored only in this browser and sent only to Anthropic.

If off, a “Check facts” button appears instead.

Skips Shorts you scroll past quickly, which saves money.
